## 2. Priority Sorting Algorithm

As mandated, uncovered student accounts are prioritized to balance **high student value** against **prolonged lack of contact**:

$$\text{Priority Rank} = \left[\text{Attended Classes in Previous Months} \downarrow \text{(Highest to Lowest)}\right] \times \left[\text{Last Connection Time} \uparrow \text{(Oldest to Newest)}\right]$$

1. **Primary Priority — High Past Class Attendees First:**
   - Students who completed the most classes represent the highest engagement and highest lifetime value.
   - Losing an 8–12 class attendee is a catastrophic retention loss; therefore, they rank at the very top of each representative's call list.
2. **Secondary Priority — Oldest Connection Time First:**
   - Within each attendance tier, leads with the oldest connection timestamp (or accounts with **No Recorded Connection**) are escalated first.
   - Accounts recently contacted via SOP are placed lower than students who have gone months without contact.
