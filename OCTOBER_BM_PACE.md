## 🎯 1. Methodology & Interpolation Rules

1. **Official Anchor Days (معالم الجدول الرسمية المعتمدة)**:
   - Days explicitly defined by management in the October calendar:
     - **Day 1**: 5%
     - **Day 3**: 11%
     - **Day 7**: 23%
     - **Day 10**: 26%
     - **Day 14**: 38%
     - **Day 17**: 43%
     - **Day 21**: 54%
     - **Day 25**: 60%
     - **Days 26–31 (Final Push)**: 64%, 76%, 83%, 90%, 96%, 102%
2. **Intermediate Days Interpolation (المتوسطات التناسبية للأيام البينية)**:
   - For all empty days and OFF DAYS, targets are calculated via smooth linear interpolation ($\Delta = \frac{\text{Next Anchor} - \text{Prev Anchor}}{\text{Span}}$):
     - **Days 1–3**: Day 2 (OFF DAY) = **8%** (Midpoint of 5% and 11%).
     - **Days 3–7**: $\Delta = \frac{23 - 11}{4} = 3\%$/day $\rightarrow$ Day 4 = **14%**, Day 5 = **17%**, Day 6 = **20%**.
     - **Days 7–10**: $\Delta = \frac{26 - 23}{3} = 1\%$/day $\rightarrow$ Day 8 (OFF) = **24%**, Day 9 (OFF) = **25%**.
     - **Days 10–14**: $\Delta = \frac{38 - 26}{4} = 3\%$/day $\rightarrow$ Day 11 = **29%**, Day 12 = **32%**, Day 13 = **35%**.
     - **Days 14–17**: $\Delta = \frac{43 - 38}{3} \approx 1.67\%$/day $\rightarrow$ Day 15 = **40%**, Day 16 (OFF) = **41%**.
     - **Days 17–21**: $\Delta = \frac{54 - 43}{4} = 2.75\%$/day $\rightarrow$ Day 18 = **46%**, Day 19 = **49%**, Day 20 = **51%**.
     - **Days 21–25**: $\Delta = \frac{60 - 54}{4} = 1.5\%$/day $\rightarrow$ Day 22 = **56%**, Day 23 (OFF) = **57%**, Day 24 (OFF) = **58%**.
     - **Days 25–31**: Given directly in image: Day 25 = **60%**, Day 26 = **64%**, Day 27 = **76%**, Day 28 = **83%**, Day 29 = **90%**, Day 30 = **96%**, Day 31 = **102%**.
